Peer-reviewed literature

For the mechanoreceptors, the four low-threshold classes are grounded in human physiology, but some molecular and anatomical studies use animal models. The simulator does not reproduce the firing rates or thresholds from any individual paper.

Scientific context and limitations

The model separates functional peripheral afferents from their underlying molecular contributors. TRPM8 and TRPV1 have the strongest supporting evidence for innocuous cold and noxious heat. TRPA1 cold transduction remains debated; TRPV3 and TRPV4 are prominent in keratinocytes; there is no confidently established separate human “TRPV2 extreme-heat receptor.” The exact recruitment thresholds depend on skin region, baseline adaptation, rate and duration of stimulation.

Peer-reviewed literature

Thermal categories are functional rather than one-channel/one-neuron identities. Human microneurography, molecular experiments, and animal models contribute different types of evidence; simulated temperature-response functions are not direct fits to human datasets.
